Symbols Nerd Font Mono
----------------------

wmux includes SymbolsNerdFontMono-Regular.woff2, converted without glyph
changes from SymbolsNerdFontMono-Regular.ttf in the official Nerd Fonts
v3.5.1 release:

https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts/releases/tag/v3.5.1

The symbols aggregate Codicons, Devicons, Font Awesome, Font Awesome
Extension, Font Logos, Material Design Icons, Octicons, Seti, Pomicons,
Powerline symbols, IEC power symbols, and Weather Icons. Those glyph sets
retain their respective MIT, Apache-2.0, CC BY 4.0, and SIL OFL 1.1 terms.
The official Symbols Only README is the authoritative attribution table and
reports Font Logos as unlicensed:

https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts/blob/v3.5.1/patched-fonts/NerdFontsSymbolsOnly/README.md
